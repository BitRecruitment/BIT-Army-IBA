Understanding Your Mindset & Resilience

Answer these 5 scenario-based questions thoughtfully. There are no right or wrong answers—we value authentic reflection, constructive resilience, and alignment with club values.

Scenario 01 • Handling Criticism

Suppose you spend weeks designing a tool or tech solution, but someone points out publicly that an existing tool already does it better, calling your work futile. How do you respond in that moment? And what do you do with the project afterward? *

Scenario 02 • Dealing with Pressure

During a live club event, a technical setup or system you were responsible for fails unexpectedly in front of the entire audience. What is your immediate reaction on-site? And how do you handle the situation with the team afterward? *

Scenario 03 • Prestige Comparison

Other clubs on campus have decade-old reputations, huge alumni networks and massive budgets, while we are starting from scratch. When your peers ask why you joined an unproven club instead of an established, prestigious one, what is your genuine answer? *

Scenario 04 • Resilience

Imagine we spend three weeks preparing our very first flagship workshop or tech showcase and only 6 people show up while an older club packs an auditorium down the hall. What goes through your head? And what is your step the next morning? *

Scenario 05 • Legacy

A year from now, when the next batch of recruits join, what is the single tangible system, culture or project you want to point to and say, “We built that from scratch when this club required us the most”? *
